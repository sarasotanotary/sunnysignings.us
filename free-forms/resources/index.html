Filing online

Florida courts accept filings online through the Florida Courts E-Filing Portal. People representing themselves can register and file there. E-filing available: $35 per filing. We e-file your completed, signed documents. Court and clerk fees are paid at cost. We do not prepare or review documents or give legal advice, and the clerk decides whether to accept a filing. Check each form for its own signing and notary instructions. Need a notary? Book a visit.
