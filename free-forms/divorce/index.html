Filing for divorce without a lawyer in Florida

Most people going through a divorce in Florida represent themselves. Download the Sarasota County divorce packets below, fill them in free, and get typing help or a notary if you want it.

We cannot tell you which forms you need or what to write on them. If you are unsure, contact the 12th Circuit's Family Self-Help program (linked on the resources page) or a Florida attorney.

Sarasota, Manatee & DeSoto divorce packets

These are the four dissolution of marriage packets the 12th Judicial Circuit uses for Sarasota, Manatee and DeSoto counties. Every form in each packet downloads from here as a blank PDF, exactly as the court publishes it, and you can fill most of them free in your browser. We cannot tell you which packet applies; the court's own description is under each title, and the flow chart in each packet walks you through it.
