Filing for divorce without a lawyer in Florida

Most people going through a divorce in Florida represent themselves. Here are the official forms, the disclosure every nonlawyer helper must give you, and typing help if you'd rather not do it alone.

We cannot tell you which forms you need or what to write on them. If you are unsure, contact the 12th Circuit's Family Self-Help program or a Florida attorney.
