How it works

Use Images → PDF to turn one or more phone photos — a scanned ID, a signed document, a receipt — into a single, properly sized PDF you can email or print. Use PDF → Images to pull the pages out of an existing PDF as individual JPG files, handy when you only need to text or upload one page.

Tip: HEIC photos (the default format on many iPhones) only preview correctly in Safari. If a photo fails to load, open it in your Photos app and share/export it as a JPG first, or switch your camera format to "Most Compatible" in Settings → Camera → Formats.

Need to combine PDFs instead? Try Merge PDF or Rotate & Reorder Pages.
