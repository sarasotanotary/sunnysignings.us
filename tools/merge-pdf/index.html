    How it works

    Add your PDF files above — from your phone's camera scans, email attachments, or anywhere else. Drag the arrows to put them in the order you want them to appear in the final document, then press Merge & Download. The combined PDF saves straight to your device, just like any other download.

    Why this matters for a notary visit: if a client sends you several separate scans (ID photo, a signed page, a supporting document), you can merge them into one tidy PDF before printing or filing — without ever emailing the files to a third-party website.

    Need something else done to a PDF? Try JPG ⇄ PDF or Rotate & Reorder Pages.
