Why these tools are private by design

Every tool on this page runs with open-source JavaScript libraries loaded straight into your browser. When you add a file, it's read and processed on your own device — it is never sent to Sunny Signings, never stored anywhere, and never passes through our servers. That matters to us as much as it does to you: our notary clients trust us with sensitive paperwork, and the right answer is tools that simply can't leak what they never receive.

Good to know: closing this tab or losing your connection won't lose your file to anyone else — because it was never sent anywhere in the first place. It just means you'd need to add it again to pick up where you left off.
