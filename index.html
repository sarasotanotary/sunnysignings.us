Questions

Common questions

How much does a mobile notary visit cost in Sarasota County?

A mobile visit is $85 in Sarasota County. It covers notarizing one packet or two individual documents, plus $10 for each additional notarization. Out-of-county, after-hours, and other special cases are quoted individually.

Which counties do you serve?

Sarasota, Manatee, Charlotte, Lee, and Collier counties in Southwest Florida.

What do I need to have ready for a notary visit?

Every signer needs a valid government-issued photo ID and must be present. Please do not sign your documents before the notary arrives.

Do you handle loan signings and closings?

Yes. We act as a mobile closing and loan signing agent for purchase closings, refinances, HELOCs and equity lines, reverse mortgage signings, and powers of attorney and affidavits.

Can you give legal advice or tell me which form to use?

No. A notary cannot give legal advice or choose forms for you. For legal questions, please speak with a Florida attorney.

How do I book?

Use the booking form on this page or call (941) 500-2495.

Can you type up my documents for me?

Yes, through our Document Preparation Service. We type LLC formation, name change, divorce, and other paperwork from the information you give us. We're not attorneys and can't give legal advice or tell you which form to use.
